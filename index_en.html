Both doors open by turning the key clockwise. The longest key is for the apartment door. The other key with teeth is for the door facing the street (front door). Parking

If you have a car, you can park in the marked spaces on the streets or in any parking lot between the blocks, including the one next to the playground. Yellow shaded areas are prohibited for parking. Keep in mind that the entire neighborhood is a “Blue Zone” and parking everywhere is paid! The zone is open every day without exception from 9:00 to 20:00. You can park in the zone for a maximum of 3 consecutive hours if you pay per hour (price 0.51 euros per hour) by sending an SMS to number 1352 and text – the car number, or via the MPARK mobile application https://mpark.bg/en/ (through it you can also pay for a 30-minute stay). You can also pay for a full day stay – price 8.18 euros, but for this you must install the MPARK mobile application. You can also pay for the Blue Zone at the grocery store, which is located on the corner opposite the upper end of the block.

Sightseeing

 

The See Garden

You can go to the See Garden on foot. It takes about 10 minutes to reach and enter it. The city beach is at a distance of ten more minutes walking.

There you can find children playgrounds, tennis courts, bars and restaurants, the Dolphinarium, the Zoo, the museum of Nature, the Planetarium (you can observe the stars and the night sky), the Aquarium and next to it the Naval museum. On the north part of the beach, you can find the Wake Park where you can ride a surf even there are no waves. There is also a skate park in the area of the Wake Park and many places serving food and drinks.

On south, the See Garden ends with the swimming pool complex and the Port where you can find many restaurants, bars and discotheques, and attractions for the small and grown children.
